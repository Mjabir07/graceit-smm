## Rules to keep brand awareness working

- **Consistency > volume.** 3 solid posts/week beats 10 then silence.
- **Same visual identity** every post (logo, colours, fonts) — that IS brand awareness.
- **80% value/proof, 20% offer.** Posts 1–6,12,14,15,19,24,28,29 build brand; 2,17,20,30 convert.
- **Localise everything** — "Sharjah," "SAIF Zone," "Northern Emirates" in captions + tags.
- **Real photos win.** Stock is fine to start; real install/team photos build far more trust.
- **One primary WhatsApp number** in every CTA — fix the two-number split first.
