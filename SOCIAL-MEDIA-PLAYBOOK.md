---

## 1. Guiding principle — social is not the lead engine, it is the trust layer

For a Sharjah B2B IT + CCTV firm, buyers convert on **Google Maps + Search + WhatsApp** (see main report). Social's job is **support**, in three specific ways:

1. **Proof** — when a buyer researches Grace before calling, active profiles with real installs + reviews reduce risk.
2. **Retargeting fuel** — content + a warm audience make Meta remarketing cheaper and better.
3. **Authority + referrals** — LinkedIn presence wins AMC/B2B trust and partner relationships.

**Do not chase followers or virality.** Chase *credibility signals that shorten the sales decision*.

---

## 2. Platform priority — where to actually spend effort

Do **not** open every platform. Ranked for this business:

| Rank | Platform | Role | Effort | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Google Business Profile (Posts)** | Local discovery + trust | Low | Closest to the buy decision; feeds Maps ranking. Technically local-SEO, run it like social. |
| **2** | **LinkedIn (Company + owner)** | B2B authority, AMC, partners | Medium | Highest-value B2B channel; decision-makers + referral partners live here. |
| **3** | **Instagram** | Visual proof (installs, before/after, Reels) | Medium | CCTV/cabling is highly visual; Reels feed Meta ads + retargeting. |
| **4** | **Facebook** | Local reach, ads base, reviews | Low | Mirror Instagram; needed as the ad account + local audience. |
| **5** | **YouTube** | Evergreen how-to/testimonials | Low (later) | SEO + remarketing asset library. Start Month 3+. |
| **skip** | **TikTok / X** | — | — | Low B2B ROI for Sharjah IT/security now. Revisit only if a clear angle appears. |

**Rule:** master #1–#4 before touching #5. One platform done well beats five half-dead.

## 4. Content pillars (rotate — never all-sell)

| Pillar | % of posts | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Authority / Compliance** | 20% | Own the "approved CCTV" niche | "How long must UAE businesses keep CCTV footage?" |
| **Proof / Installs** | 20% | Visual credibility | Before/after cabling, install timelapse |
| **Education** | 20% | Rank + save + share | "3 signs your office PC is dying" |
| **Testimonials / Reviews** | 15% | Social proof | Screenshot of a 5★ Google review |
| **Service / Offer** | 10% | Direct response | "Free CCTV site survey this week" |
| **Team / Behind-the-scenes** | 10% | Human trust ("Spirit of Service") | Meet-the-technician |
| **Industry insight** | 5% | Authority | UAE IT/security trend snippet |

**Golden ratio:** ~80% value/proof, ~20% direct offer. Hard-sell every post = dead account.

## 5. Posting cadence (realistic for an SME — sustainable beats ambitious)

| Platform | Cadence | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | 2 Posts/week + photos weekly | Offer/update/review + install photos |
| LinkedIn | 3 posts/week | Authority, case, education, hire/partner |
| Instagram | 3–4/week (feed + Reels) | 1–2 Reels + 2 feed/carousel |
| Facebook | mirror Instagram | Cross-post + local group shares |
| Stories (IG/FB) | 3–5/week | Quick installs, polls, behind-scenes |

**Batch weekly.** One 2-hour session = a week of content. Capture install photos/video **on every job** — that is 80% of your feed for free.

## 7. Ten short-form Reel/video scripts (shoot on a phone)

1. **Cable cleanup (15s)** — messy rack → clean rack. Text: "Your server room doesn't have to look like this." CTA: WhatsApp.
2. **"Is your CCTV approved?" (20s)** — 3 on-screen questions; end: "If unsure, you may fail renewal. We're Sharjah Police approved."
3. **Install timelapse (15s)** — camera mount → live feed on phone. "From survey to live feed."
4. **Data recovery reveal (20s)** — dead drive → recovered files. "Don't panic. Don't DIY. Call us."
5. **3 signs your PC is dying (20s)** — quick cuts. "Fix it before you lose data."
6. **What an AMC gets you (25s)** — fast list overlay. "One fixed monthly cost. One team."
7. **Technician tip (15s)** — one real tip (e.g. "restart weekly, not never").
8. **Office setup before/after (20s)** — empty office → wired + running.
9. **Customer thank-you (15s)** — real client saying one line + 5★.
10. **"We answer when you call" (15s)** — phone rings, tech picks up fast. "The Spirit of Service."

**Production rules:** vertical 9:16, captions burned in (sound-off viewing), first 2 seconds = hook, one idea per video, logo end-card + WhatsApp CTA.

## 9. Tools & workflow (lean stack)

- **Scheduling:** Meta Business Suite (free, IG+FB) + Buffer/free tier for LinkedIn/GBP.
- **Design:** Canva (templates for carousels/quote cards/infographics — build a branded kit once).
- **Video:** CapCut (phone editing, captions).
- **Capture:** technicians photograph/film **every** job (get customer consent) → shared album → weekly batch.
- **Links:** one WhatsApp click-to-chat link + a simple link-in-bio.
- **Reviews → content:** every new Google review → screenshot → post (closes the loop with Part 16).

## 10. Social KPIs (tie to business, not vanity)

| Metric | What it tells you | Target (first 90 days) [ASSUMPTION] |
|---|---|---|
| Profile → WhatsApp clicks | Real intent | Track from day 1; grow monthly |
| GBP Posts views + calls | Local discovery | Rising Maps calls |
| Reel/video views + saves | Content resonance | Identify top formats, repeat |
| Follower growth | Reach base (secondary) | Steady, not the goal |
| Reviews generated via social prompts | Trust compounding | Feed the Part 16 flow |
| Retargeting audience size | Ad efficiency fuel | Big enough to run Meta remarketing (~Month 2) |
| Leads *attributed* to social | Business impact | Tag source in CRM |

**Never report "likes" to management.** Report **WhatsApp clicks, attributed leads, reviews, retargeting reach.**

## 11. 90-day social roadmap

- **Month 1 — Foundation:** claim + build all 4 profiles (identical NAP), branded assets, start job-photo capture, publish the 4-week calendar, first reviews-to-posts.
- **Month 2 — Rhythm + retargeting:** consistent cadence, build a Canva template kit, launch Meta retargeting off the warm audience, double down on best Reel format, LinkedIn outbound to partners.
- **Month 3 — Authority + scale:** start YouTube (repurpose Reels + testimonials), first case-study posts, seed GBP Q&A, measure attributed leads, cut dead formats, plan next quarter from data.

---

## 12. What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't open TikTok/X "because everyone says so" — no local B2B ROI now.
- ❌ Don't buy followers or fake reviews — penalties + zero trust value.
- ❌ Don't post only offers — 80% value/proof or the account dies.
- ❌ Don't run different names/addresses per platform — kills local SEO.
- ❌ Don't let profiles go dark for weeks — inactive profile < no profile for trust.
- ❌ Don't measure success by likes — measure WhatsApp clicks + attributed leads.
